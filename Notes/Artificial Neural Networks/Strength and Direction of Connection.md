
A simple explanation of what the synaptic weight controls between an input and the neuron.

---

## Everyday Analogy

Think of each input ($x_j$) as a person talking to the neuron, and the **weight** as the volume of that person’s voice and whether they are encouraging or discouraging the neuron.

---

## Strength

**Strength** means how loud that person’s voice is.

- A large weight → the input has a strong influence on the neuron’s decision.
- A small weight → the input barely matters.

---

## Direction

**Direction** means whether the person is saying “Yes, fire!” or “No, don’t fire!”

- Positive weight → the input pushes the neuron toward activating (excitatory).
- Negative weight → the input pushes the neuron toward staying quiet (inhibitory).

---

## Simple Summary

When we say the weight controls the **strength and direction of the connection**, we simply mean:

> “How strongly does this input affect the neuron, and does it try to turn the neuron on or turn it off?”
